## Consent
# Study title: Study on the perception of real and virtual objects
Please read this information notice carefully. We recommend that you save or print this page.

Main researcher: Pierre Dragicevic, Research Scientist at Inria Bordeaux, France - pierre.dragicevic@inria.fr

Research goal: This study aims to enhance our understanding of how real and virtual objects are perceived in an augmented reality context, when visual cues are used to help distinguish between real and virtual objects. More details about the study’s objective and hypotheses will be given at the end of this study.

What we expect from you: You will be asked to answer questions related to the previous experiment on the perception of real and virtual objects, to which you participated about 10 days ago. You will first see a sequence of icons and words, some referring to objects you have seen, others to new objects. Each time, you will be asked to remember whether you saw the object as real, virtual, or if it is new. Then, for each object you identified as real or virtual, you will be asked to explain what cues you used to inform your choice. You will then be given three questionnaires: 1) a self-assessment questionnaire about your perceived memory abilities in daily life; 2) a self-assessment of your familiarity with augmented reality; 3) A brief sociodemographic questionnaire. This study should take about X minutes to complete. We ask you to complete the study without any distraction or pause. There will be one or more easy attention check questions which are trivial if you pay attention to the study, and whose sole purpose is to filter out bots and participants who click randomly or rush through the study.

Eligibility: To take part in this study, you must speak English, be 18 years of age or older, and have normal or corrected-to-normal vision. You must have participated in the previous study on the perception of real and virtual objects.

Your rights to withdraw from the study at any time: Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary and you may stop at any time, without providing a reason. If you do so, your data will be deleted. However, we will not be able to pay you if you do not complete the study.

Your rights to confidentiality and privacy: We (researchers) do not have access to your personally identifiable information. All your responses will be stored anonymously. We will check your open-text responses to confirm that no identifiable information is provided. After you paste your completion code, you will not be able to access or delete your data. We will delete your Prolific ID from our data after we verify your response. Once our research project is completed, all anonymized data will be made publicly available on an open science platform. 

Potential benefits: You will receive £X for participating in this study, under the condition that you complete the study, do not rush through the study, and answer the attention check questions correctly.

Risks and discomforts: Your participation in this study does not present any foreseeable risks beyond those experienced in daily life.

Publication: This research will be published in a scientific journal or at a conference. The anonymized data will be made publicly available on an open science platform.

Right to ask questions: Please contact Pierre Dragicevic (pierre.dragicevic@inria.fr) if you have any questions, complaints, suggestions or concerns about this research. Concerns can also be filed to the ethical committee of the University of Bordeaux, which has approved this study: cer.ub@u-bordeaux.fr. For data protection concerns, contact the data protection officer at Inria: dpo@inria.fr.
