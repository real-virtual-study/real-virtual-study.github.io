- Part 1 : 
    - Questionnaires :
      1 - A presence questionnaire, or a subset of the questionnaire will be used to assess the participants’ sense of presence.
      2 - An embodiment questionnaire, or a subset of the questionnaire will be used to evaluate the degree to which the participant felt embodied in the self-avatar.
      3 - A simulator sickness questionnaire, or a subset of the questionnaire will be used to measure any discomfort or motion sickness the participant may have experienced during the session.
      4 - A study purpose questionnaire, designed to check whether the participant was convinced by the cover story of the study or had any doubts about the experiment’s true purpose.


- Part 2 : 
  - Questionnaires : 
    - Source confusion test 
    - - Source identification questionnaire (adapted from Bonnail et al., 2024): The participant will see a sequence of sentences, some of which were seen in Part 1, while others are new sentences. For each sentence, they will be asked to indicate if they already saw it during Part 1 of the study, and if so, whether it was spoken by themselves, by their self-avatar, or by the virtual interlocutor. They will also rate their confidence in each response. Finally, they will be able to explain their answer.

- Everyday Memory Questionnaire (EMQ) (adapted from Royle & Lincoln, 2008): The participant will self-assess their everyday memory abilities, including the frequency of common forgetfulness.
- Familiarity with Augmented Reality Technologies Questionnaire (adapted from Moffat, 2001 and Petiot et al., 2025a)
- Sociodemographic Questionnaire: we will ask participants to report their gender, age, education level.
